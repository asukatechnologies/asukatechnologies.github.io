Dando In testing

Every run, scored, replayed and understood.

Dando is a mobile app for practical shooting competitors. It brings the whole run into one place: the timer, the video, the targets and the score. Afterwards you can see exactly what happened on a stage and where the time went, instead of piecing it together from a timer readout and a phone video.

Capture the run

Connect your shot timer and Dando records every shot as you shoot. Your phone records video at the same time, and so can a head- or chest-mounted action camera, so you get the shooter’s view as well as the spectator’s.

Score it

Photograph your targets and Dando helps you score each hit, then works out your total with your penalties included. You can score runs on established stages or in free practice, where you set up the targets yourself.

Replay it shot by shot

Every shot is marked on the video and lined up with your splits. Step through the run, see where each shot landed, and find the moments that cost you time.

Understand your performance

Follow your times and scores across runs and stages. See which stages suit you, which don’t, and whether you’re getting better.

Compare

Put a run next to your own earlier attempts at the same stage, or next to runs other competitors have shared, and see where the difference is.

Share when you’re ready

Runs stay on your phone until you choose to post them. Export a run as a video with your splits on screen, ready to share.

Built for the range

Dando works without a signal and keeps everything on your phone, so poor coverage at the berm doesn’t stop you.

Availability

Dando is in internal testing and will come to iOS and Android.
