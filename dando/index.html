Dando In testing

Your practical shooting runs, in one place.

Dando is a mobile app for practical shooting competitors. It brings scoring, video and analysis together, so you can see what happened on a stage and where the time went.

Score your runs

Record and score each run, on established stages or in practice.

Review on video

Watch each run back, shot by shot.

Understand your performance

Follow your times and scores over time and see where you’re getting better.

Compare

See how a run stacks up against your earlier runs and against other competitors.

Built for the range

Works offline, and your runs stay on your phone until you choose to share them.

Availability

Dando is in internal testing and will come to iOS and Android.
