Delete your Dando account

Dando, a mobile app by Asuka Technologies LLC

You can delete your Dando account at any time, in the app or by email. Deleting your account removes your account and everything in it. To keep your account and delete only some of your data, see Delete some of your data.

In the app

  1. Open Dando and sign in.
  2. Open the menu and tap Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Choose when:
    • Delete in 30 days: your account is hidden from everyone straight away. Sign back in within 30 days to restore it. After that it is permanently deleted.
    • Delete immediately: your account is erased now and cannot be restored. You type your handle to confirm.

We email you to confirm the deletion.

By email

If you can’t use the app (you’ve uninstalled it, lost your phone, or can’t sign in), email privacy@asukatechnologies.com with the subject Delete my Dando account.

  • Send it from the email address on your account, and include your handle if you know it.
  • Say if you want it deleted immediately. Otherwise we schedule the deletion in 30 days, so you can still change your mind.
  • Shortly after you write, we reply to the address on the account to make sure the request is yours, and delete the account once you confirm.

What is deleted

Your account and everything in it: your profile, runs, series, videos, photos, posts and follows. Runs saved on your phone are removed by the app once the deletion is complete, or straight away if you uninstall Dando.

What we keep

  • Records of your payments and subscriptions, and of your acceptance of our Terms, which we keep for tax, accounting and legal purposes. These include your email address.
  • A record that your account was deleted, and when.
  • Copies in our backups, for a limited time until they are replaced.

See our Privacy Policy for more.

Subscriptions

  • App Store: deleting your account does not cancel it. Only you can, in your Apple Account. Apple explains how: cancel a subscription from Apple.
  • Google Play: it stops renewing when you delete your account. Restoring your account does not restart it.
  • Card: it stops renewing, and restoring your account before the billing period ends turns renewal back on. Deleting immediately cancels it.

Changed your mind?

If you chose to delete in 30 days, sign back in to Dando before then and restore your account.

Delete some of your data

You can also delete some of your data and keep your account.

  • A posted run: open the post and choose Delete Post. The post, and the videos and photos posted with it, are deleted from our servers.
  • A run on your phone: open the run and choose Delete Run. If you also posted it, delete the post as well.
  • Your profile photo: in Settings, tap your photo and choose Remove photo.
  • A loadout saved to the cloud: open it and choose Delete from cloud. Posts that show the loadout keep it until you delete those posts.

To have any other information deleted, email privacy@asukatechnologies.com from the address on your account and tell us what you’d like removed. We reply to confirm the request is yours before we act on it.
