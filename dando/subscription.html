Manage your Dando subscription

Dando, a mobile app by Asuka Technologies LLC

Where you manage your Dando subscription depends on how you pay: by card, through Google Play, or through the App Store.

Not sure how you pay?

Open Dando, open the menu, tap Settings, then Manage subscription. It shows how you pay and opens the right place. Your receipt emails also say who charged you: Stripe for card, Google Play, or Apple.

Paid by card

Card subscriptions are processed by Stripe. Sign in to Stripe’s billing page with the email address you subscribed with. Stripe emails you a sign-in link; no Dando password is needed.

Manage your card subscription

There you can:

  • update your card,
  • see and download your receipts and invoices,
  • cancel, or turn renewal back on before the period ends.

In the app: Settings › Manage subscription › Manage card subscription.

When you cancel, you keep access until the end of the period you have paid for. Uninstalling the app does not cancel your subscription.

Paid through Google Play

Google Play manages these subscriptions, including cancelling and refunds. Open your Google Play subscriptions and choose Dando, or see Google’s help on cancelling a subscription on Google Play.

Paid through the App Store

Apple manages these subscriptions, including cancelling and refunds. Apple explains how to cancel a subscription from Apple.

Receipts and billing help

Card subscriptions: Stripe emails a receipt after each payment, and every past receipt and invoice is on Stripe’s billing page above.

For billing questions, a charge you don’t recognize, or a refund request, email support@asukatechnologies.com with the subject Dando billing. Send it from the email address you subscribed with, and include the receipt number if you have it.

Card payments are non-refundable, except that we refund charges made in error to your original payment method. See section 4.2 of our Terms of Use. You can also dispute an unauthorized charge with your card issuer.

Google Play and App Store subscriptions: receipts and refunds come from Google or Apple, through the links above.

Deleting your account

Deleting your account affects card and Google Play subscriptions differently from App Store ones. See Delete your Dando account.

During the beta, card subscriptions are free and no card is charged.
